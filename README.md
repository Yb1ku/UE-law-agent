# EU Legal Assistant 🇪🇺 

AI Chatbot designed to give support with EU legislation and regulations. Its knowledge base includes: 

- **GDPR** — data protection rights and obligations
- **AI Act** — AI system requirements, prohibited practices, conformity
- **Data Governance Act** — data sharing frameworks and data intermediaries
- **Data Act** — rights to access and share data from connected products
- **Cyber Resilience Act** — cybersecurity requirements for digital products
